# Yipsilon

Research and code at the intersection of **behavioral science, HCI and data science**.

I build experimental systems and analyze behavioral and text data to study how people make decisions and interact online.

## Current work

**Online decisions under information overload** — An individual research project examining how information load and decision guidance affect information search and goal-consistent product choice. I develop the online shopping experiment and its behavioral-data workflow using Next.js/TypeScript, Qualtrics, Supabase and Python.

**Online discussion dynamics and measurement bias** — Collaborative research on reply timing and conversation structure in Wikipedia and Reddit discussions, including text classification and checks on how measurement choices affect the patterns we observe.

## Tools & methods

- **Data & ML:** Python, R, SQL, PyTorch, NumPy, pandas, scikit-learn; statistical modeling, experimental design and cross-validation.
- **NLP:** Text mining, Japanese tokenization, sentiment analysis and text classification.
- **Web & workflow:** Next.js/React, TypeScript, HTML/CSS, Supabase/PostgreSQL, Qualtrics, Git/GitHub, Linux, Docker and LaTeX.
